## Brand & Style
The design system embodies the quiet confidence of an established editorial review desk rather than the ephemeral hype of a modern AI utility. Designed for ambitious students, career changers, and experienced job seekers, the interface establishes an atmosphere of rigorous craftsmanship, academic authority, and thoughtful counsel. It avoids the visual tropes of generative tech—such as synthetic neon glows, dynamic radial gradients, floating glass panels, and playful emojis—in favor of structural clarity, precise typography, and tactile paper-like materiality.

The design movement combines **Minimalism** and **Modern Editorial Publishing**:
- Generous structural whitespace and deliberate asymmetry frame career insights as high-value critiques rather than automated system outputs.
- Sharp architectural grids and clean 1px hairline divisions organize complex resume breakdowns into calm, digestible assessments.
- Restrained interactions reward precision; feedback is direct, neutral, and clear.

## Colors
The palette is rooted in print editorial values and quiet archival tones. It eschews pure synthetic neutrals in favor of organic, warm paper-inspired shades:

- **Base Canvas (`#FAF8F4`)**: A warm, unbleached cotton paper tone that eliminates eye fatigue during sustained revision sessions.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure, crisp white used strictly for content sheets, metric modules, and elevated review panels.
- **Primary Accent (`#1F6F54`)**: Deep forest green, applied conservatively for final calls to action, high-tier match metrics, and verified states.
- **Secondary Accent (`#2D8A66`)**: Muted pine green, utilized for supportive progression meters, secondary confirmations, and safe feedback tags.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#D9822B`)**: Warm amber for critical suggestions, phrasing improvements, and caution flags.
- **Critical Semantic (`#C84B31`)**: Muted terra-cotta red, deployed solely for severe resume gaps, formatting errors, or missing requirements.
- **Text Primary (`#1A1A1A`)**: A deep near-black carbon with warm undertones, ensuring peak typographic contrast without digital harshness.
- **Text Secondary (`#6B6B6B`)**: Balanced charcoal gray for editorial meta-labels, subheadings, and contextual hints.
- **Hairline Border (`#E6E2DA`)**: A subtle, warm stone border applied uniformly at 1px to structure content cards without heavy drop-off.

## Typography
Typographic pairings celebrate the contrast between literary tradition and utilitarian efficiency:

- **Headlines (Newsreader)**: Deployed for core titles, section narratives, score overviews, and qualitative assessment statements. Its fine serifs and balanced italics impart dignity and human editorial consideration.
- **Body & Controls (Inter)**: Handles all actionable elements, analytical data points, inline revisions, and parsed resume content. Clean horizontal tracking and robust legibility ensure that critical hiring feedback remains unmistakably clear.
- **Uppercase Usage**: Reserved exclusively for small labels (`label-sm`), metadata categories, and status indicators with a `letterSpacing` of `0.04em` to mimic bibliographic tagging.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a disciplined centered-canvas model designed to focus the user's attention on document analysis:

- **Constrained Shell**: All central workflows—resume upload, line-by-line critique, keyword matching, and section scoring—live within a centered container strictly capped at a max-width of `1100px`.
- **Desktop Grid**: A 12-column layout with `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters and an outer section boundary of `2rem` (`32px`). Multi-pane critique views split naturally into an 8-column resume viewer and a 4-column AI diagnostic panel.
- **Tablet & Mobile Adaptation**: At viewports narrower than `840px`, the desktop split collapses into a clean tabbed presentation (Document View vs. Analysis View), gutters tighten to `1rem` (`16px`), and margin boundaries reduce to `1rem` (`16px`).
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Sub-sections and score clusters are spaced with deliberate breathing room using `space-lg` (`24px`) and `space-xl` (`40px`), reinforcing an uncluttered reading environment.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in this system is tactile and paper-weight inspired, rejecting dramatic z-index layering or artificial backdrops:

- **Surface Planarity**: The canvas (`#FAF8F4`) sits directly beneath pure white document cards (`#FFFFFF`). Spatial differentiation is primarily communicated through uniform `1px solid #E6E2DA` borders.
- **Ambient Shadowing**: Elevation does not float high into space. Rested elements feature an ultra-soft, warm whisper of elevation:
  - *Rested Card*: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.02);`
  - *Active Popover / Dropdown*: `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.06), 0 1px 4px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.04);`
- **Focus & States**: Elevating an item on hover never introduces glowing halos or dimensional jumps. Interactions are indicated strictly by border transitions (to `#1F6F54`) or subtle background shifts.

## Shapes
Shapes emphasize structure and purposeful tactility without becoming overly rounded:

- **Cards & Analysis Containers**: Set to `12px` border-radius (`rounded-lg` under roundedness level 2). This provides balanced softness without losing the rectangular stability of physical paper.
- **Interactive Elements (Buttons, Text Inputs, Segmented Controls)**: Set to `8px` border-radius (`0.5rem`). This establishes a clear visual distinction between structural framing and actionable affordances.
- **Status Tags & Pill Badges**: Use fully rounded caps (`9999px`) only for small categorical metadata and metric capsules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#1F6F54`, label text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `8px`. State changes: hover `#175540`, active scale neutral. No heavy gradients or internal bevels.
- **Secondary (Outline)**: Background transparent, border `1px solid #E6E2DA`, text `#1A1A1A`. Hover shifts background to `#FAF8F4` and border to `#1A1A1A`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Borderless, text `#6B6B6B`, hover text `#1A1A1A` with a subtle under-accent or light paper tint.

### Cards & Modules
- Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), `1px solid #E6E2DA` border, `12px` radius. Padding strictly follows `space-lg` (`24px`).
- Internal section dividers use `1px solid #E6E2DA` running edge-to-edge without drop-shadow offsets.

### Feedback Chips & Badges
- **Match Score / Positive**: Background `#EBF5F0`, text `#1F6F54`, border `1px solid #C4E2D5`.
- **Attention / Caution**: Background `#FDF6EE`, text `#D9822B`, border `1px solid #F5DCBF`.
- **Issue / Negative**: Background `#FAF0EE`, text `#C84B31`, border `1px solid #F2CBC4`.
- Shape: 4px padding-y, 10px padding-x, rounded-full (`9999px`), `label-sm` typography.

### Input Fields & Upload Drops
- **Input Fields**: Crisp white background, `1px solid #E6E2DA` border, `8px` radius. Typography set to `body-md` in `#1A1A1A`. Focused state: border turns `#1F6F54` with no secondary fuzzy ring.
- **Document Dropzone**: Canvas background `#FAF8F4`, enclosed in a 1px dashed `#E6E2DA` border with `12px` radius. States during drag-over switch border to solid `#1F6F54` with zero canvas warping.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Checkboxes: 16px square, `4px` radius, `1px solid #E6E2DA`. When checked, background fills with `#1F6F54` and a white SVG checkmark.
- Radio Buttons: 16px circular, 1px border. Checked state displays a solid inner dot of `#1F6F54` on white.

### Lists & Line-by-Line Critique Panels
- Critique lists feature a 1px hairline separation between suggestions.
- Interactive annotations link directly to resume bullet points with a muted underline in `#D9822B` or `#1F6F54`, presenting advice via unobtrusive popover panels framed in crisp `#FFFFFF`.
